About Us

Our Story

Nova had spent decades as the infamous space pirate Null, but grew weary of seeing the harsh reality of company systems chewing up and spitting out the next generation. With the grand sums she had stolen Nova transformed her beloved ship into a new kind of home she could share with the weary. At first serving as a place of rest and hiding for those fleeing the system, she saw the need for good food and good company, and Nova's Nutrition was born. Now half a century later people journey from far systems to enjoy this infamous pirate's legacy.
